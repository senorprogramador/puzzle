# Code of Conduct

Puzzle UI aims to be a welcoming, safe, and respectful community for everyone
who participates in good faith.

## Our pledge

We value the dignity, rights, and contributions of all participants. Community
members should be treated fairly regardless of their background, identity,
experience, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to:

- Communicate respectfully and honestly.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and receive constructive feedback in good faith.
- Take responsibility for their actions and correct harm where possible.
- Credit sources and respect the privacy and boundaries of others.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention,
threats or encouragement of harm, impersonation, sharing private information
without permission, and other conduct that threatens the safety or well-being
of community members are not acceptable. Repeated unwanted contact after a
clear request to stop is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement responsibilities

The project is maintained by an individual maintainer; no separate conduct
committee or moderation team has been established. The maintainer is
responsible for receiving reports, reviewing relevant information, and
deciding on an appropriate response. Reports will be handled as privately as
reasonably possible. No response-time guarantee or formal appeal process has
been established.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces, including repository issues,
pull requests, discussions, and project-related events. It also applies when
someone is representing Puzzle UI in an official capacity.

## Reporting

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainer privately using a contact method
listed on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/senorprogramador),
if available. Do not post details about a person's report in a public issue or
discussion. This repository does not currently publish a dedicated private
conduct-reporting address; if no private contact method is available, request
one without sharing incident details.
