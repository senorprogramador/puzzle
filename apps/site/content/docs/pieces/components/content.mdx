Content sections render editorial and media data provided by the application. They do not fetch articles, infer permissions, host video, or manage event registration.

## Media and data-driven composition

Use the [Video Section](/docs/pieces/components/video-section) for controlled
video collections, [Resource Section](/docs/pieces/components/resource-section)
for searchable learning libraries, and [Event Section](/docs/pieces/components/event-section)
for dated programs. Provide stable IDs, meaningful image alternatives, captions,
and display dates appropriate to the user's locale.

Layouts use Puzzle Theme tokens and reflow at small widths. Caller-provided
media, filters, and actions should also remain responsive.
