Business sections give organizations reusable ways to present people, work, services, and contact details. All records, claims, contact endpoints, and media are provided by the application.

**Scope:** This is a category overview, not a separate component API. Every public component linked from this page has a Stable API; consult its reference for detailed props and variants.

Compose [Accommodation Details](/docs/pieces/components/accommodation-details)
with [Amenity List](/docs/pieces/components/amenity-list) and existing
[Hero](/docs/pieces/components/hero) and
[GallerySection](/docs/pieces/components/gallery-section). The complete
[Accommodation Discovery example](/docs/pieces/components/accommodation-discovery)
also includes guest reviews and a CTA. The [Hospitality Booking Flow](/docs/pieces/components/hospitality-booking-flow)
adds a caller-data-driven availability form, rates, stay summary, and house
rules without adding a booking engine.
The [Restaurant Experience](/docs/pieces/components/restaurant-experience)
composition combines caller-provided menu, hours, location, and reservation
presentation.
Use [Host Profile](/docs/pieces/components/host-profile) for one hospitality
host rather than duplicating TeamSection profile cards, and
[Service Options](/docs/pieces/components/service-options) when included or
optional service status is part of the presentation.

## Composition and accessibility

Compose a profile, services, team, and contact section to tell one coherent
organization story. Keep heading levels logical, identify informative images,
label maps and forms supplied through slots, and use real `mailto:`/`tel:` or
application routes. Sections use semantic markup, responsive layouts, and
Puzzle Theme tokens.

See [Specialized & Interactive](/docs/pieces/components/specialized-interactive)
for venue and booking layouts.
