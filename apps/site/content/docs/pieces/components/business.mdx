Business sections give organizations reusable ways to present people, work, services, and contact details. All records, claims, contact endpoints, and media are provided by the application.

<Badge content="Beta" color="info" /> **API maturity: Beta.**

## Components

| Component | Purpose | Reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| About Section | Explain organization purpose and history. | [About Section](/docs/pieces/components/about-section) |
| Team Section | Introduce people and roles. | [Team Section](/docs/pieces/components/team-section) |
| Services Section | Describe services and outcomes. | [Services Section](/docs/pieces/components/services-section) |
| Case Studies Section | Share customer work and results. | [Case Studies](/docs/pieces/components/case-studies-section) |
| Contact Section | Combine contact methods, department contacts, and form/map slots. | [Contact Section](/docs/pieces/components/contact-section) |
| Careers Section | Present open roles and workplace information. | [Careers Section](/docs/pieces/components/careers-section) |
| Partners Section | Show partner organizations. | [Partners Section](/docs/pieces/components/partners-section) |
| Awards Section | Present awards and recognition. | [Awards Section](/docs/pieces/components/awards-section) |

## Composition and accessibility

Compose a profile, services, team, and contact section to tell one coherent
organization story. Keep heading levels logical, identify informative images,
label maps and forms supplied through slots, and use real `mailto:`/`tel:` or
application routes. Sections use semantic markup, responsive layouts, and
Puzzle Theme tokens.

See [Specialized & Interactive](/docs/pieces/components/specialized-interactive)
for venue and booking layouts.
