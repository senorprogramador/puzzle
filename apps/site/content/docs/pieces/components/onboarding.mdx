## Interactive onboarding

The example controls its selected step and completion state in the consuming React component. Use the arrow keys, Home, and End to move through enabled steps; disabled steps are skipped and focus follows keyboard selection.

<Preview fit="device" code={`<OnboardingSection steps={steps} currentStep={currentStep} onStepChange={setCurrentStep} />`}>
  <OnboardingInteractiveExample />
</Preview>

Supported variants: `steps`, `checklist`, `progress`, `wizard`, `cards`, `split`, `welcome`, `centered`, `horizontal`, `vertical`, and `compact`.

## Accessibility

- The section is labelled by its heading; the progress indicator exposes a progressbar name and value.
- Completion and progress are announced through a polite status message.
- Provide stable step IDs. Disabled steps use native disabled buttons when step selection is enabled.
- Steps remain application-controlled; update `currentStep` in `onStepChange` and move focus only through the provided keyboard navigation.
