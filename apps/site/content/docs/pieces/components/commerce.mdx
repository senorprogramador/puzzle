Commerce sections arrange data supplied by the store application. Product availability, pricing calculations, cart state, checkout, inventory, and payment remain outside Pieces.

<Badge content="Beta" color="info" /> **API maturity: Beta.**

| Component | Use case | Reference |

## Responsive behavior and theme

Product grids and details adapt to available space. Use the Preview device
control to inspect mobile and desktop layouts; keep product images sized and
described by the application. Commerce sections consume semantic Puzzle Theme
tokens and work inside scoped themes.
