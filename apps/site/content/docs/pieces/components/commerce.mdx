Commerce sections arrange data supplied by a store or restaurant application. Product availability, pricing calculations, cart state, checkout, inventory, orders, and payment remain outside Pieces.

**Scope:** This is a category overview, not a separate component API. Every public component linked from this page has a Stable API; consult its reference for detailed props and variants.

## Responsive behavior and theme

Product grids and details adapt to available space. Use the Preview device
control to inspect mobile and desktop layouts; keep product images sized and
described by the application. Commerce sections consume semantic Puzzle Theme
tokens and work inside scoped themes.

The [Hospitality Booking Flow](/docs/pieces/components/hospitality-booking-flow)
composes availability search, rate plans, a stay summary, and house rules. The
application supplies availability and price data and owns all calculations,
reservations, and payment behavior.

The [Restaurant Menu](/docs/pieces/components/restaurant-menu) composes
caller-supplied menus, categories, dishes, prices, dietary labels, and allergen
information. Filtering only matches supplied dietary-tag IDs; it does not
infer ingredients or determine allergen suitability. Ordering and payment are
application responsibilities.
The [Restaurant Experience](/docs/pieces/components/restaurant-experience)
example composes the menu with opening hours, location, and a reservation
request section. Reservation submission and availability remain application
responsibilities.

The [Hospitality Overview](/docs/pieces/components/hospitality-overview)
compares accommodation, restaurant, and shared hospitality sections. Gift
voucher purchase links are supplied by the application; Pieces does not
provide checkout or payment.
