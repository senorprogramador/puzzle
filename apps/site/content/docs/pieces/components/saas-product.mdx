Puzzle Pieces provides section compositions for SaaS teams explaining product value, integrations, use cases, trust, pricing, code, and product direction. The application supplies the claims, data, routes, billing rules, and media.

<Badge content="Beta" color="info" /> **API maturity: Beta.** See each reference for exact props, item types, variant behavior, and accessibility requirements.

## Composition and customization

Compose a product story from a showcase, feature, and pricing section rather
than treating Pieces as a complete landing-page template. Use UI `Tabs`,
`Accordion`, `Card`, and `Button` where their behavior fits; supply screenshots
with meaningful alternative text. The sections consume Puzzle Theme tokens and
adapt to the preview's mobile/tablet/desktop widths.

For framework boundaries and server/client examples, see
[Next.js and TanStack Start integration](/docs/pieces/framework-integration).
